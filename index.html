HOW TO PLAY

  • You are Bot 2969. Make words on the board from your 7 tiles and outscore the Bot.
  • Drag a tile onto the board, or tap a tile then a square. Tap a placed tile to take it back. Press PLAY.
  • Keyboard: click a square and type the word. Space flips across/down, Backspace undoes, Enter plays.
  • Your first word covers the ★. Every word after that must join one already on the board.
  • DL/TL double or triple a letter, DW/TW the whole word. Use all 7 tiles for a +35 bonus.
  • It ends when the bag is empty and someone uses their last tile. 2:00 a turn; miss three turns in a row and you forfeit.
  • Your points rank on the leaderboard, win or lose, and today's tile bag is the same for everyone.

Free play never touches a wallet, never asks for a seed phrase, and never moves a Bot.
